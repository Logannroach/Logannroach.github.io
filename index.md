## Hello, I’m Logan Roach 👋

I’m a senior at the Georgia Institute of Technology studying Business Administration with a concentration in Finance and a minor in Computer Science. With a strong academic foundation (GPA: 4.0), I’ve held roles at Morgan Stanley and Invesco where I applied data-driven methods and technical tools like Python, Power BI, and SQL to drive meaningful insights in wealth and capital markets. I currently lead the Media & Telecom sector of Georgia Tech’s $2M student-managed investment fund, and serve as VP of Membership Development in the Scheller Business Ambassadors program.

My work blends financial rigor with technical fluency, and I’m passionate about solving problems at the intersection of investment, innovation, and technology.

### Client Name Mapping System  
**Role:** Developer | **Tools:** Python, VBA  
**Timeline:** Jan 2022 – Present  
**Company:** CIBC Commodities Desk  

While working on the Commodities Desk at CIBC, I encountered a persistent issue in our internal name identification process. Client identifiers were mismatched across trading platforms, causing trade reconciliation delays and introducing risk in our reporting and compliance workflows. To resolve this, I designed and implemented an internal name mapping update system using Python and VBA.

This tool automatically standardizes and cross-references client identifiers pulled from multiple systems. It integrates seamlessly into daily trading operations, eliminates name mismatch failure rates, and ensures high-fidelity client data. As a result, the tool significantly improved operational accuracy, reduced manual correction work, and bolstered trust in internal and client-facing analytics.

The project is still actively maintained and has been credited by the team with streamlining one of the most error-prone areas of our daily reporting infrastructure.
